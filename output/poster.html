01 / Creative systems

Jeevan Saiesh Paramkusam is a Creative Technologist building the future of work by combining graphic design, video editing, AI agents, and automation. He builds automated creative systems that can generate videos, posters, and social content, alongside intelligent business workflows for lead generation, sales, customer support, recruitment, finance, knowledge management, reporting, e-commerce, and operations.

02 / Long-term goal

His long-term goal is to help businesses replace repetitive manual processes with intelligent AI-powered systems while preserving human creativity where taste, judgment, and perfection matter.

03 / The plan

He plans to build a high-value AI automation business and retain premium human creative services for clients who specifically value exceptional design and video craftsmanship.
